# Can I use Portainer in a production environment?

Yes you can; however, for business critical apps we recommend Portainer Business Edition. With a purchase of Portainer Business Edition, you’ll get access to all the extended features that are available in BE, as well help with onboarding, prioritized support, and a dedicated Customer Support Engineer.
